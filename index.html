Gymnema Sylvestre

Gymnema Sylvestre is a traditional herb commonly used in Ayurvedic wellness practices. GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid)

GABA is a naturally occurring neurotransmitter that helps regulate communication within the nervous system. It is associated with the body's natural calming processes and is included in some dietary supplements marketed for relaxation and mental wellness. Research into the effects of oral GABA supplementation is still developing.
